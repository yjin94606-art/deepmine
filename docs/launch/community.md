# Deepmine 1.1 launch copy

## Repository description

A local pixel-art game where you dig up what you made with AI. Recorded tokens power the drill. Your work fills the mine.

## Launch post

**What did you make with today's tokens? I built a game that lets you dig it up.**

At the end of a day with a coding assistant, I wanted to see what all those tokens had gone into. In Deepmine, I open a project and start a little brass drill. When it hits a drawing, the rock splits and the picture comes up in its own colors. A sound file leaves a different trace. I can see the three notes of a door chime in its waveform.

My favorite part is coming back a few days later. I can scroll up the shaft to Tuesday and find that drawing again, even if I've forgotten its filename.

Deepmine 1.1 runs on your computer. Pick a recent Codex or Claude Code project to dig up your own work. [Get Deepmine on GitHub](https://github.com/yjin94606-art/deepmine)

I'll keep working on the feel of the drill, support for more workflows, and finds that look like the things you actually made. What would you want to recognize the moment it came out of the rock? [Tell me on GitHub](https://github.com/yjin94606-art/deepmine/issues)
